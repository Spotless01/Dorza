DORZA is a Ghanaian door manufacturing and carpentry standardization brand focused on producing high-quality, reliable, and professionally crafted doors while creating sustainable opportunities for local craftsmen and young people.

Our operations are designed are to simplify the process for homeowners, developers, contractors, and property owners by creating a more organized and dependable way to access quality wooden doors

DORZA connects skilled carpenters under one unified brand and ensures every door produced meets strict quality, durability, and design standards. Through certification, training, and production partnerships, we are building a trusted ecosystem where customers receive consistent value and craftsmen receive consistent work and growth opportunities.

DORZA is committed to promoting confidence in quality locally crafted wooden doors by supporting structured production, skilled craftsmanship, and higher finishing standards within the industry.

🎯

Mission

To standardize door production, empower carpenters, and provide customers with reliable, high-quality Ghana-made doors through innovation, certification, and sustainable employment systems.

🌍

Vision

To become the most trusted and globally recognized door brand built from Ghana, creating a unified network of certified craftsmen and delivering high-quality doors across Africa and beyond.
